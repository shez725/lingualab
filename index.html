About LinguaLab

LinguaLab is a learning tool for linguistics students. It turns the syntax trees you draw on paper into something you can watch being built, tap, and explore.

Trees marked Linguist Verified have been reviewed by the founder. Trees marked AI Generated are drawn by an AI model following the standard framework and have not yet been reviewed, so check them against your course notes.
